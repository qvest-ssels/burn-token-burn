synth

For two different visitors: first-timers with an empty adapters table, and contributors/demo-recorders who need reproducible data.

synth generates a complete set of fake coding-agent telemetry, so you can try report, status and the rest before you have accumulated any usage of your own — and so the behaviour you are looking at is reproducible. Scenarios range from a quiet week to burst and exhausted.

If adapters came back empty

You installed it, ran token-finops adapters, and nothing showed as found — maybe you run a tool that isn't supported yet, or your tool just hasn't written enough telemetry. This is the genuinely good use of this page for a first-timer: try the whole CLI on fake data in one command, without touching anything of yours. The scenario below (exhausted) is a good one to reach for, because it guarantees something interesting to look at in report and status right away.

Scenarios, for contributors and demo recordings

Full scenario list (steady, burst, exhausted, weekend, fresh, quiet, subagent-heavy) and what each one is meant to demonstrate is in docs/SYNTH.md — this is also how every recording on this site was made.

Try it

$ token-finops synth --out ~/demo-home --scenario exhausted --print-env

Generating a demo data set and the --print-env one-liner.

What to notice: --print-env prints the environment assignments that point the CLI at the generated data instead of your real one. Evaluate that line in a shell and every other command on this site works against the demo data set; open a new shell and you are back to your own. Nothing you already had is modified — token-finops never writes to a coding agent's data store.

Recordings are asciicast v2 files committed in docs/casts/ — play them locally with asciinema play <file>. This page ships no analytics and no tracking.
